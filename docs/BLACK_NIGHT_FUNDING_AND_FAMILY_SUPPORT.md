## Four distinct kinds of support — never mix them up

1. **Personal/family aid:** charitable assistance, food, utility or rent payments, transportation, or emergency support. Usually not repaid, but often restricted to a specific bill and eligibility rules.
2. **Business grants/prizes:** non-repayable business funding, often competitive, restricted to eligible expenses, geography, business stage, or industry. May require reports and receipts.
3. **Equity investment:** cash into Black Night Consulting One LLC in exchange for ownership or a future ownership right (SAFE/convertible instrument). It is not a grant and dilutes ownership.
4. **Paid remote work / sweat equity:** compensation for actual services or work in exchange for agreed equity. This is not passive income and needs a written agreement defining role, hours, vesting, ownership, IP, and termination.

## How to approach investors without personal cash

Build a concise investment package for Black Night Finance Studio:
- A working demo or clickable prototype, even if narrow.
- One clear first customer and problem, not an everything-platform pitch.
- Evidence of demand: interviews, letters of intent, pilot users, waitlist, or paid pilot.
- 12-month milestones and a lean use-of-funds budget.
- Founder role, ownership table, current liabilities, and company status accurately disclosed.
- A short deck and one-page summary.
- A specific ask: grant, paid pilot, accelerator, SAFE/convertible investment, or co-founder with defined responsibilities.

Do not offer equity vaguely in return for "help." For any equity or SAFE, have a qualified startup attorney review valuation/cap, discount, dilution, vesting, IP assignment, control rights, investor rights, and exit provisions. Do not promise guaranteed returns.

## Remote paid work versus investor equity

If the user means they personally want to work remotely for an investor or startup and receive a share, target paid fractional operations, finance, grants, budgeting, financial modeling, compliance, or business-development roles with an equity component. A true employee should not be asked to work indefinitely for speculative equity alone. Require written pay, equity percentage or number of units, vesting schedule, valuation method, role, expected hours, and what happens if the relationship ends.

## Scams and red flags

- No legitimate federal grant requires a gift card, crypto payment, wire fee, or payment to "release" the award.
- Be wary of unsolicited messages promising personal grants from wealthy donors, celebrities, or government agencies.
- Do not share SSN, bank login, tax return, or identity documents until the organization is verified through its official domain and a legitimate application process.
- Grants.gov is primarily a federal grant application portal for eligible organizations and entities; it is not a general personal free-money program.
- Never pay a grant finder to reveal secret grants.
- Do not borrow on credit cards to meet a grant match or pay accelerator fees.
